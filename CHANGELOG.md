# Changelog

**Reading this file.** Every change that ships is logged, internal work included,
and each entry opens with a tag saying who it reaches:

| Tag | Visible to |
| --- | --- |
| `[user]` | someone **using** Goal Tracker: web UI, emails, observable behaviour |
| `[ops]` | someone **running** it: env vars, migrations, deploy steps |
| `[api]` | a **programmatic contract**: the spreadsheet import format, database schema, configuration |
| `[internal]` | nothing externally observable: refactors, CI, tests, docs, plumbing |

`**Breaking changes:**` comes first in a milestone and names the contract it broke.
The format is in [`docs/changelog-conventions.md`](docs/changelog-conventions.md).

### Collected changes — October 4, 2026

**Breaking changes:**
- [ops] **Database schema / migrations**: migration `0039_report_rules` runs on startup and is one-way. It converts each saved Report Definition to the new model, then drops the `report_definition_roots` and `report_definition_filters` tables and the `depth` and `owner_filter_id` columns of `report_definitions`. A definition with only filters becomes Report rules: an "Owner is" rule for its Owner filter and one "is any of" rule per filtered Dimension. A definition with roots becomes a hand-picked list, frozen to the Goals it selected at upgrade: its roots walked down accepted links to its Depth, then kept by its Owner and Dimension filters. One whose Goals all fail its filters is left with an empty list and drafts no Goals. It adds `report_definitions.mode` and the `report_rules`, `report_rule_values` and `report_definition_goals` tables. A build from before it can't read the upgraded database, so back the database up before upgrading (#147).

**New features:**
- [user] New goal, on Home and the Goal list, opens its own page at `/goals/new` in place of the Goal list's pop-up form. Fill in the Title and So What and Create Goal lands on the new Proposed Goal's page. If a submit is refused, the form comes back with what you typed, a list of the problems at the top, and each problem marked on its field. The page is a plain form, so it works without JavaScript (#129).
- [user] The New goal page has a "How you'll know" section for adding Milestones (Name, Date) and Metrics (Name, Unit, Baseline, Target, Target date) as the Goal is created. "+ Milestone" and "+ Metric" add a row and × removes one; without JavaScript there is one row of each. A Metric's direction comes from its numbers: a target below the baseline means down, above means up, and only a Metric holding steady, with target equal to baseline, asks for a Direction. A row left blank is ignored; a partly filled row has each missing value, unreadable date and non-numeric number marked on its field, and the rest of the form keeps what you typed (#133).
- [user] The New goal page has a "Contributes to" field for picking any number of parent Goals as the Goal is created. Type to search Goals by title. Each Goal you pick becomes a chip showing its Health (or its Lifecycle when it has none) and its title, and × removes it. A hint names the Owners who'll be asked to accept; a parent you own links at once. Opening `/goals/new?parent=<id>` arrives with that parent already picked. Without JavaScript the field is a list where you can select several Goals. The field is optional (#131).
- [user] The New goal page opens with What and why, its So What hinted with the customer problem, then Delivery: pick Dated or Ongoing, each explained, with the delivery date showing once Dated is picked, and a Check-in cadence of Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly or a Custom number of days. A Custom cadence that isn't a whole number of days above 0 is refused beside it, and a date posted with Ongoing is ignored (#130).
- [user] The New goal page has a "Where it fits" section for giving the Goal its Dimension values and Fields as it is created: a dropdown for a Dimension that takes one value, checkboxes for one that takes several, a box to add a new value to an Extendable Dimension, and an input of each Field's type. All of it is optional. Opening `/goals/new?parent=<id>` arrives with the parents' values ticked, leaving out Retired ones and, where parents disagree in a Dimension that takes one value, choosing none there; only what is still ticked when you create the Goal is given to it. A refused submit keeps exactly what you chose and typed, with a bad Field value or two values in a one-value Dimension marked on its input (#132).
- [user] The New goal page has a "Ready to activate" card beside the form, which stays in view as you scroll. It shows the activation checklist (So What, Owner, Dated with a delivery date or Ongoing, a Milestone or Metric, and a value in each required Dimension and Field), a progress bar and "n of m", and it updates as you type. The card holds two buttons in place of Create Goal: **Create and activate**, which creates the Goal Active and is disabled until every item is done, and **Save as Proposed**, which needs only a Title and So What. Without JavaScript the card shows the checklist as the page loaded and Create and activate stays enabled. The server always checks: if the Goal can't be activated, the form comes back listing each unmet rule and nothing is saved (#134).
- [user] A Proposed Goal's Owner finishes defining it on the New goal form: Finish defining on the Goal page opens `/goals/<id>/define`, filled in from the Goal, where one submit changes the So What, Kind, cadence, Dimension values and Fields, adds Milestones, Metrics and parents, and can activate it. Existing Milestones, Metrics and parents show read-only, a Pending parent isn't requested again, and a Goal that would close a cycle isn't offered as a parent (#135).
- [user] A Report lists the Goals that entered or left it since its latest publication, each linking to the Goal, with Added or Left and why: picked by hand or now matches the rules; finished before this report's baseline, removed by hand or no longer matches the rules. It compares against the latest publication whatever baseline you pick, shows on the draft's preview, the publication and its print page, is frozen with the publication, and is left out when no Goal entered or left. The Markdown export doesn't carry it (#160).
- [user] New report on the Reports page opens a builder at `/reports/new` in place of the list's inline form. Name the report, give it an Introduction, and choose what's in it: **Goals matching rules** or **Goals I pick**. A rule tests a Dimension, the Owner, the Lifecycle, the Health or Top-level, with "is", "is any of" or "is not"; a Goal must meet every rule and any of a rule's values. Fields describe Goals and aren't offered for rules, and neither is a Retired Dimension or value. "+ Add rule" adds a rule, × removes one, and **Also include** and **Leave out** add or take away Goals by hand. Each Goal list searches every Goal, at any level, by title, and "+ Fields to show" chooses the Fields shown beside each Goal. **Show matches** lists the Goals the first draft would hold. Save lands on the new report's draft. A refused save comes back with everything you entered and each problem beside its input. Without JavaScript, each rule is a set of lists, Add rule reloads the form with one more rule, and each Goal list is a list where you can select several Goals. The Root Goals, Depth and filters form is gone (#149).
- [user] The Risks page can be scoped like the Goal list: **Everyone | Mine** keeps just the Goals you Own or are a Delegate on, and a Dimension value select keeps the Goals with that value. The scope lives in the address (`?mine=1`, `?value=`), combines with a group card's filter, and Back returns to the earlier scope; the header count and the group cards count only the Goals in scope, while the top bar's Risks count stays org-wide (#140).
- [user] The report builder at `/reports/new` has a **Matches N Goals** rail beside the form that refreshes as you type: a flat list of the Goals the report would select on its draft, by title, each with its Health (or its Lifecycle before a Check-in), "added" beside a Goal only Also include brings in, and "+ n more · m left out" past the first 10. A rules report with no usable rule yet asks you to add one. Without JavaScript, **Show matches** re-renders the builder as typed with the same rail, saving nothing (#152).
- [user] A saved Report Definition can be edited: **Edit definition**, in the draft's header and its "Goals in this report" panel, opens the builder filled with its Name, Introduction, rules or picked Goals, Also include, Leave out and Fields, with the matches rail beside it. Saving changes only the draft; publications stay as they were published. Only the definition's creator or an Admin sees the link and may edit; anyone else gets 403. A saved rule on a since-Retired Dimension or value still shows on the edit page, so saving keeps it (#151).
- [user] Anyone but a Goal's Owner can suggest a parent for it, with an optional note: **Suggest a parent** in the Goal page's menu, and the Risks page's Fix for someone else's Unaligned Goal (a Delegate's included), lead to a form listing the Active and Proposed Goals it could contribute to. The Owner decides it from Home's Requests, where it counts in what needs them, or from a new Suggested parents list on the Goal page. Accepting requests the link as if they had, already accepted when the suggester owns the parent; Decline offers Undo. The suggester can withdraw it, and it closes as no longer applying when its link comes about another way or its Goal or parent is Done or Cancelled. Each suggestion and its outcome is in the Goal's History, under All (#144).
- [user] Anyone who can't check in on a Stale or Path-to-Green-overdue Goal can **Nudge** it from its Risks row: its Owner and Delegates (anyone Departed left out) get a one-off email asking for a Check-in, with a link to the Check-in form. A Goal is nudged at most once a day in the org's timezone, from anyone; after that, its row says **Nudged today by** whoever did, while its Owner and Delegates keep Check in. Nudge outranks Suggest a parent on a row that's also Unaligned, and a Goal nobody is left to check in on keeps its other Fix. A refused Nudge says why on a page of its own, and each Nudge is in the Goal's History, under Check-ins and All. "Reminder" still means the weekly email (#143).
- [user] A Goal's History now shows every change to its "contributes to" links: a request, its acceptance or rejection, a removal, and an Undo of a rejection or removal, each with who did it and when. Each change is on both Goals' History, phrased for the side it's on ("Requested to contribute to *Reduce outages*" on the child, "*Migrate displays* asked to contribute to this" on the parent), with the other Goal linked. A new **Links** chip, after Ownership, lists these changes and the Goal's Parent suggestions (#145).
