# Changelog

**Reading this file.** Every change that ships is logged, internal work included,
and each entry opens with a tag saying who it reaches:

| Tag | Visible to |
| --- | --- |
| `[user]` | someone **using** Goal Tracker: web UI, emails, observable behaviour |
| `[ops]` | someone **running** it: env vars, migrations, deploy steps |
| `[api]` | a **programmatic contract**: the spreadsheet import format, database schema, configuration |
| `[internal]` | nothing externally observable: refactors, CI, tests, docs, plumbing |

`**Breaking changes:**` comes first in a milestone and names the contract it broke.
The format is in [`docs/changelog-conventions.md`](docs/changelog-conventions.md).

### Collected changes — October 21, 2026

**Breaking changes:**
- [ops] **Sessions**: the session cookie now carries when the person signed in and when they were last seen, so existing sessions are signed out once and everyone signs in again (#414).

**New features:**
- [user] Every signed-in page sits in a new frame: a sidebar on the left replaces the teal top bar. It holds the app's name (linking Home), a Search that asks the Goal list, the nav with icons and counts, My org (the people directly under you, each linking to their org on Goals, shown only to someone with people under them), and your Name and initials at the foot, opening a menu with Theme and Sign out (#390).
- [user] At 900px and below the sidebar becomes a 48px strip with the app's name, Home's count and a Menu that opens the rest as a sheet, without script (#390).
- [user] Each published Report's row on Reports, and a publication's page, has a **Download** menu: **Markdown (.md)** or **PDF (.pdf)**, for the Report's latest publication on the list. It replaces the publication page's Markdown and Print / PDF buttons (#388).
- [user] A PDF download is a real PDF file, the publication's print page rendered by a headless Chromium, at `/reports/{id}/publications/{pub}/pdf`. Without Chromium, the menu's item reads **PDF (print from browser)** and opens the print page, and the `/pdf` link redirects there (#388).
- [ops] `GOAL_TRACKER_CHROME` names the Chromium that renders PDF downloads; unset, the app looks on `PATH` for `chromium`, `chromium-browser`, `google-chrome` or `google-chrome-stable`. Startup logs which it uses or that PDF downloads fall back to the print page, and stops when `GOAL_TRACKER_CHROME` isn't there or can't run. At most two PDFs render at once, each within 30 seconds (#388).
- [user] A Report has **Report Delegates**. Its creator or an Admin adds them by email in the new Delegates section of the Report's draft page, and removes them there; everyone who opens the Report sees who they are. A Report Delegate may change the Report, curate its narrative and publish it, as its creator may, and may hand its open Action Items to a new owner, but can't add or remove Report Delegates (#434).
- [ops] Migration 0054 adds the `report_delegates` table; it runs on startup with the others (#434).
- [ops] `GET /healthz` answers `200` `ok`, signed out, while the database answers within a second, and `503` when it doesn't, for a load balancer or orchestrator to probe (#417).
- [ops] Every request gets one `INFO` access line once it's answered, with its `method`, `route` (the pattern it matched, such as `GET /goals/{id}`, or `unmatched`), `status`, `bytes`, `duration_ms` and `request_id`. Every record logged while handling the request carries the same `request_id`, which the response returns in `X-Request-ID`. An incoming `X-Request-ID` of 1 to 64 of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` is reused; any other is replaced (#417).
- [user] A Report's draft page has a **Report log**: every change to its Report Definition, newest first, with who made it and when in the company's timezone. It records the Definition's creation, each edit to its title, introduction, how it selects, its rules, its picked Goals, its Also include and Leave out, and the Fields it shows (each before → after in words, such as "Title: EU MBR → EU monthly review" or "Rule added: Owner is in the org of Priya"), each Report Delegate added or removed, each change to the next publication's narrative (Highlights picked, moved or dropped, and which sections' notes changed), and each publication. Anyone who can open the Report reads it. The newest 20 entries show, and the rest are behind Show all. Saving an unchanged Definition or narrative logs nothing (#435).

**Security:**
- [user] A session now ends 30 days after sign-in, or after 7 days without a request, however it is used; a request at least a day after the last re-issues the cookie to keep it from idling out (#414).
- [user] Signing out now ends every session you have on the server, on this browser and any other, so a copy of the session cookie taken earlier no longer signs anyone in (#414).
- [user] The app refuses, with a 403, a form post or other change another site's page sends on a signed-in person's behalf, and no other site can show its pages in a frame to trick a click onto one of its buttons; every response now also sends `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy: same-origin` (#415).
- [user] The Goal table's **Download CSV** no longer writes a cell that a spreadsheet would run as a formula. A Goal title, Field value or other cell starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return is written behind a `'`, so `=1+1` downloads as `'=1+1` and `-5` as `'-5`. The import drops that `'` again, so importing an unchanged download still changes nothing; any other leading `'` is kept (#424).
- [internal] The domain now checks who may change a Goal instead of relying on the web handler: activating a Goal, marking it Dated or Ongoing, setting its cadence, and adding Contributors, Metrics and Milestones each refuse anyone the Goal page refuses, and editing a title or Why it matters refuses anyone but the Owner or an Admin. A spreadsheet import makes these changes as the importing Admin, and only to the Goals it creates (#420).
- [internal] The Goal page asks the domain whether to offer the Check-in form and the value and Field forms, so the page and the commands can't disagree (#420).
- [ops] `GOAL_TRACKER_TRUSTED_PROXIES` names, as comma-separated CIDRs or IPs, the reverse proxies whose `X-Forwarded-For` the password sign-in throttle reads. A sign-in from one of them counts against the right-most address in the header that isn't a trusted proxy, so clients behind one proxy no longer share a single 50-failure limit. A sign-in from any other address counts against that address, whatever header it carries. Unset, the default, `X-Forwarded-For` is ignored as before. An entry that is neither a CIDR nor an IP stops startup with an error naming the variable, and startup logs the trusted proxies (#425).
- [user] Signing in through your organization now finds your Account by who the organization's sign-in says you are, not only by your email: your first sign-in links the Account to you, a later one still finds it after your email changes, and someone given an email that once belonged to another person is refused with a sentence to ask an Admin instead of being signed in as them (#426).
- [ops] Migration `0055_account_oidc_subjects` adds a nullable, unique `oidc_subject` column to `accounts`; existing Accounts link on their next OIDC sign-in, and a refused sign-in logs a warning naming the Account, not the subject (#426).
- [user] A request body over 1 MiB, or an import's upload over 5 MiB, is refused with 413 Request Entity Too Large, so a huge request can't tie up the app (#416).
