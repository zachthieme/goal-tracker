section: Architecture
- [internal] One module, `internal/web/htmx.go`, answers htmx and plain posts: `isHTMX` is the only reader of `HX-Request`, and `answerSwap`, `answerRedirect`, `answerPushedPage` and `answerUnswapped` are the only code that sets an `HX-` response header or the 200 htmx needs to swap a refusal in. The Check-in, Draft Highlight, narrative and Goal list handlers ask it, and every request gets the same answer as before (#610).

section: Testing
- [internal] Each htmx answer is tested with and without `HX-Request: true`, for its status, its `HX-` headers and a plain redirect's 303 `Location` (#610).
