section: Improvements
- [user] The Reports list's Draft column ("N changes") no longer counts a Goal whose only change since the last publication is a new Milestone, so it agrees with the draft, where such a Goal takes one line rather than an exception block. The list is ordered by that count, so such a Report can move down it (#633).
- [user] A Parent digest now reads a child's Health at the start of the recipient's week as a Report reads a Goal's Health at its baseline: a child On Hold then had no Health. So a child On Hold at the start of the week and resumed Yellow or Red this week is named as worsened to that Health, where before it was compared with the Health it had before it was put On Hold. Report output is unchanged (#633).

section: Architecture
- [internal] A Report's `goalHistory` is the one reader of a Goal's Check-ins against a baseline: `healthBefore` gives the Health at a moment, which `priorHealth` and the weekly digest both use (the digest's own `healthAt` is gone, and it reads its children through `goalHistories`). The draft keeps the Goals it judged changed on the `Report`, unfrozen, so the Reports list counts its changes without reading Check-ins, Date Slips or Milestones again for each published Definition (#633).

section: Testing
- [internal] A query-count test pins that the Reports list (`GET /reports`) reads Goals' Check-ins, Date Slips and Milestones only as often as drafting its Definitions does, and grows per Definition by only the Definition and its last publication (#633).
