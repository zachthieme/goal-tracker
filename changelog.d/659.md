- [user] A pending Handoff no longer goes unseen by whoever started it: the Goal page says "Handoff to <Name> pending" under its Owner, and Pending handoffs lists the Handoffs you started under "Handoffs you started". Whoever started a Handoff, or an Admin, can **Cancel handoff** from either, which keeps it in the Goal's History as cancelled; History now names who cancelled a Handoff, Reassign and Mark departed included, and a second Hand off while one is pending says whom it is to and to cancel it first (#659).
- [ops] **Database schema / migrations**: migration `0002_handoff_cancelled_by.sql` adds the nullable `handoffs.cancelled_by`, the Account that cancelled a Handoff. It is additive and applied on start; a Handoff cancelled before it records no one (#659).
