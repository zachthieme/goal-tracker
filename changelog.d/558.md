section: Architecture
- [internal] A No change is judged once: one batched read gathers what it is judged from, a single Goal being a batch of one, and the Goal page builds the same facts from what it read. The verdict carries the refusal, the overdue Milestone and the off-target Metrics, and `Milestone.OverdueOn` is the one overdue rule every surface judges by. Off-target Metrics are judged from every reading read at once, so a No change or a Check-in no longer runs a query per Metric (#558).
