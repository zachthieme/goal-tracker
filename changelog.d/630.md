section: Architecture
- [internal] Who gets which weekly email is decided in the domain: `Service.QueueWeeklyEmails` composes the Check-in reminders and the parent digests, then records the week's slot and queues them in one transaction, under the job `domain.WeeklyEmailsJob` ("weekly emails", unchanged). `notify` keeps only the schedule (`Weekly`, `Scheduler`); `notify.Notifier` is gone, and `AllOpenActionItems`, `QueueEmails` and `EmailComposer()` are no longer exported from the Service. The emails, their recipients and their order are unchanged (#630).
