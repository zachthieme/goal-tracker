section: Improvements
- [ops] Every background job (the email outbox, the prune, the weekly emails and the directory sync) logs a failure the same way: `msg="background job failed"` at `ERROR`, naming the `job`, the `err` and the count of `failures` in a row, the first at once and then, while it keeps failing, only after a wait that doubles from a minute up to an hour; its recovery logs `msg="background job recovered"` at `INFO` once. This replaces `msg="email outbox: deliver"` and `msg="prune stale rows"` (logged at every failure), `msg="weekly emails failed"`/`msg="weekly emails recovered"`, and the directory sync's own failure line for its hourly run (an Admin's **Sync now** still logs its own). A failed run is still tried again at the job's next turn, and the weekly emails' error names the slot it was for (#616).
- [ops] A weekly slot missed while the server was down is sent as the server starts, rather than within a minute of starting (#616).
- [ops] At `GOAL_TRACKER_LOG_LEVEL=debug`, each background job logs `msg="job started"` and `msg="job stopped"` with its `job`, and the server logs `msg="database closed"` once every job has stopped (#616).

section: Architecture
- [internal] One runner in the new `internal/jobs` package starts, paces, logs and stops the background jobs, which `main` registers with it; `Service.RunEmailOutbox`, `Service.RunPrune`, `Scheduler.Run`, `Scheduler.Poll` and `Sync.RunEvery` are gone, the outbox's wake channel is `Service.EmailWake`, and the prune job's run is `Service.PruneStaleAndLog` (#616).
