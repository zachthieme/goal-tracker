section: Testing
- [internal] `make test` runs in about half the time (a mean of 155s against 308s, back to back on 48 cores). It turns off pointer checks for modernc's SQLite alone (`-gcflags=modernc.org/...=-d=checkptr=0`), keeping `-race` everywhere and checkptr in our own packages, and runs four of a package's tests at once (`-parallel 4`). The harness's migrated template database is now cached in `$TMPDIR/goal-tracker-testdb` under a hash of the migrations and shared by every test binary and later runs, and `internal/db`'s upgrade tests copy it rather than migrating from empty, taking that package from 97s to about 28s (#525).
