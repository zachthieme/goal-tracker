section: Architecture
- [internal] Each Follow-ups row now carries what its viewer may do with its Goal, judged once in the domain by the Goal rules: `FollowUpRow.Permissions` (the Goal page's `GoalPermissions`, built by one function both pages call, without No change), `Nudge` (the Nudge rule, now one function `Service.Nudge` evaluates too), `OwnedByViewer`, and each Schedule conflict's `RelatedPermissions` on its parent. The row's Fix picks among those, and the page no longer judges rights or Lifecycles itself. `GoalPermissions` gains `WritesCheckins`, whether the viewer writes the Goal's Check-ins whatever its Lifecycle. No query is added (#615).
