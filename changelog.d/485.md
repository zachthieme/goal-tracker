section: Bug fixes
- [user] Starting a Handoff twice at once (a double-submit) no longer leaves the Goal with two pending Handoffs: one is started and the other refused as already pending. Accepting and rejecting one Handoff at once now has one outcome: before, both could succeed and leave the Goal on the new Owner with the Handoff recorded as rejected (#485).
- [ops] Migration `0059_one_pending_handoff` adds a unique index allowing one pending Handoff per Goal. On a database that already holds two pending Handoffs of one Goal it fails, naming `handoffs.goal_id`, and changes nothing; set all but one of them to `cancelled` by hand, then start the app again (#485).
