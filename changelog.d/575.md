section: Architecture
- [internal] Each Goal command's rule for who may make it, and in which Lifecycle, is one pure function in `internal/domain/goalrules.go` that the command and the Goal page both evaluate, pinned by a parity test; "Owner only" is a single rule, and `GoalPermissions` gains `Activate`, `EditMetrics`, `DecideSuggestions`, `NoChange` and `LifecycleChanges`, with the page's open suggestions in `GoalPage.OpenSuggestions` (#575).
