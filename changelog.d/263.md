section: New features
- [user] Goals and Risks can be scoped to a Chain beside Everyone and Mine. Someone with anyone under them gets **My Chain**: the Goals owned by them or anyone below them. Anyone can pick another person's Chain, by Name with their email, and the page then reads "Priya Raman's Chain", in the Goal count and in the Risks scope toggle. A Goal is in a Chain by its Owner only, so a Delegate doesn't bring one in, and an Ownerless Goal stays in its Departed Owner's Chain. The Chain is part of the address (`?chain=`), so it survives grouping, a reload and a shared link. A Chain naming no one falls back to Everyone, and so does a malformed one. An empty Chain says so in a sentence. With no Managers (no directory sync), neither page offers a Chain (#263).
