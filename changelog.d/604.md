section: Improvements
- [user] The weekly Parent digest counts its past week in the Timezone of the parent Owner it goes to, or the company's when they have none, so whether a child went Yellow or Red or recorded a Date Slip "this week" is judged on the recipient's own calendar. A child under two parents whose Owners are in different Timezones can be in one digest and not the other (#604).

section: Architecture
- [internal] The Parent digest reads through one domain page loader, `Service.WeeklyDigests`, which returns typed facts per recipient and child; notify only writes the subject and body. It runs 6 or 7 queries however many Goals and links there are, where it used to read each Owner's link requests and each Goal's children, and each child's Check-ins, Date Slips, Freshness and parents, one at a time; a child is now read and judged once however many parents it has. `querycount_digest_test.go` pins the count. A new query, `ListPendingLinks`, reads every pending link request at once, and `notify.New` drops its timezone parameter (#604).
