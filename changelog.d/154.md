- [user] A Report's draft says what it reads changes since in a chip in its header: "Changes since **last publication · Sep 22**" by default once the Report has been published, "Changes since **30 days ago · Sep 4**" before then, or "Changes since **Sep 4**" for a date you picked. The chip's menu offers Last publication (once there is one), 30 days ago, and Pick a date… with an Apply button, and works without JavaScript. It replaces the baseline date form and the "Showing what changed since the previous publication" sentence. The chosen baseline stays in the page's address (`?baseline=YYYY-MM-DD`), so saving the narrative, publishing, and closing an Action Item from the draft all keep it (#154).
