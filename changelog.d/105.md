section: New features
- [user] A Goal's page shows a Health strip above its History: one cell for each of its last 11 Check-in periods (weeks by default, or the Goal's own cadence), oldest on the left. Each cell takes the Health of the last Check-in in that period, in the Health's colour and shape. A period the Goal was Active with no Check-in is a dashed, empty cell, and a period before it became Active or while it was On Hold, Done or Cancelled is blank. Each cell has a text equivalent ("Week of 28 Sep: Yellow"), and a line under the strip sums it up, so a skipped week shows at a glance (#105).
