section: Improvements
- [ops] `cmd/seed` takes `-manifest <path>` and `-manifest-at <RFC 3339>`: after seeding it writes the e2e manifest, the Goals and people each end-to-end spec acts on, picked by role and judged at `-manifest-at` (default: the `-end` instant), as indented JSON. Without `-manifest` nothing more is written (#613).

section: Testing
- [internal] The e2e specs read their Goals and actors by role from the seed's manifest (`e2e/manifest.ts`, the `manifest` fixture) instead of querying SQLite tables with `seedLookup`, so a schema change no longer breaks e2e through a spec's SQL. The choosers move out of `TestE2EScenarioSituations` into `seed.BuildManifest`, which reads the seeded company only through the domain `Service` (a new `Service.Accounts` lists every Account), and the test now asserts on the manifest's picks for both templates, failing with the role's name when one has none. The suite no longer needs Node 22.13 for `node:sqlite` (#613).
