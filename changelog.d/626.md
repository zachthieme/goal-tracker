section: Architecture
- [internal] A Milestone as the Goal page lists it, and a Goal's current Health, come from the domain: `GoalPage` gains `ListedMilestones` (each a `domain.ListedMilestone` with its `PriorDates` and `Mark`, New and Overdue judged in the domain on the Goal's timezone), `PriorDueDates` and `Health`, and the new `Service.GoalListings` gives the Goal list, the goal table and the Delegates page each Goal's latest Check-in, current Health, freshness, Incomplete and prior due dates. Prior dates have one rule (`priorDatesOf`, replacing `goalHistory.priorDates` and web's two copies) and current Health one (`currentHealth`: the latest Check-in's while Active). The Delegates page now reads its Goals in a fixed number of queries instead of two per Goal. Every page shows what it did before (#626).
