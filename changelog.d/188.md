section: New features
- [user] A Goal's Owner and Delegates can log Draft Highlights on its page between Check-ins: a note and an optional kind (Insight, Accomplishment, Miss or none), while the Goal is Proposed, Active or On Hold. The Goal page lists the pending ones, oldest first, with who logged each and when, and a Delete for each; nobody else sees them or can log or delete one. The next Check-in form opens its Highlights with one row per pending Draft Highlight, ticked to Keep, its kind and note editable. Those kept become the Check-in's Highlights. The rest, and any kept with the note blanked, are discarded, and the Check-in's History entry lists them under "Discarded Draft Highlights". A Draft Highlight logged after the form opened waits for the next Check-in, and No change leaves them all pending. Reports only ever see a Check-in's Highlights (#188).

section: Infrastructure
- [api] Database schema: migration `0041_draft_highlights` adds a `draft_highlights` table holding each Draft Highlight: its Goal, kind (`''` for none), note, who logged it and when, and, once a Check-in discards it, that Check-in (`discarded_checkin_id`). A kept or deleted Draft Highlight's row is deleted. It's additive and runs on startup (#188).
