section: Improvements
- [user] The refusals of a parent suggestion now name the Lifecycles that take one in Lifecycle order: "a parent can only be suggested for a Proposed or Active Goal", "only a Proposed or Active Goal can be suggested as a parent", and "the Goal or its suggested parent is no longer Proposed or Active", where each used to say "Active or Proposed" (#603).

section: Architecture
- [internal] The Lifecycle is one module in `internal/domain/lifecycle.go`: one table of the five Lifecycles, in order, says which take Check-ins, which have ended, which take a parent suggestion and where a Check-in may move each. `Lifecycles`, `LifecycleTakesCheckins`, `LifecycleEnded`, `LifecycleTakesDraftHighlights`, `LifecycleTakesMilestones`, `LifecycleSuggestable` and `LifecycleChangesFrom` read it, and so do the refusal sentences that list Lifecycles. The separate "ended" checks (`ended`, `hasEnded`, the admin page's and the seed manifest's), `milestonesAddable`, `suggestable` and the literal Lifecycle lists in report rules, the report builder and the Goal list's filter now ask it. A Check-in's Lifecycle move is judged once, by its rule, and `planLifecycleChange` only checks the move's reason or outcome (#603).
