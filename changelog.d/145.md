section: New features
- [user] A Goal's History now shows every change to its "contributes to" links: a request, its acceptance or rejection, a removal, and an Undo of a rejection or removal, each with who did it and when. Each change is on both Goals' History, phrased for the side it's on ("Requested to contribute to *Reduce outages*" on the child, "*Migrate displays* asked to contribute to this" on the parent), with the other Goal linked. A new **Links** chip, after Ownership, lists these changes and the Goal's Parent suggestions (#145).

section: Infrastructure
- [api] Database schema: migration `0038_link_events` adds an append-only `link_events` table, one row per link change: its child and parent Goals, the kind (`requested`, `linked`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `rejection-undone`, `removed`, `removal-undone`), who made it and when. Every link command writes its row in the transaction that makes the change (#145).
- [ops] Migration `0038_link_events` back-fills the log once, on startup, from the existing `links`, `rejected_link_requests` and `link_removals` rows. A link accepted before the upgrade shows as one "linked" entry dated by its request, since who accepted it and when was never kept; an Undo from before it is put down to whoever rejected or removed the link. Nothing to do but deploy (#145).
