section: Architecture
- [internal] One web module, `answerRefused` (`formrefusal.go`), answers every refused command. A handler states the refusal page's heading and way back, and where its form comes back in place: `onPage` for a form on another page, through the one-time `gt_refused` cookie, or `asPage` for a form that is the page, plus an optional htmx fragment. The module applies the error-kind mapping, the htmx split and the fallbacks to the refusal page once. `writeCommandResult`, `writeFormResult`, `refuseGoalForm`, `writeDelegateResult`, `writeDraftHighlightResult`, `renderDraftHighlights`, `writeReportDelegateResult`, `writeCommentError`, `writeRefusedHandoff`, `renderSuggestParent`, `renderNoChangeRefusal`, `renderCheckinFormError` and `refuseActionItem` are gone. The cookie's helpers move from `actionitemrefusal.go` to form-refusal names, and a Goal page form carries back only its own inputs (#634).
