section: Architecture
- [internal] A Report publication now freezes its facts, words and links included, in a versioned publication record of its own (version 1), separate from the Report view model, so rewording a fact later no longer changes how an earlier publication reads. Publications from before this change can't be read: a database holding any must be reseeded (no installs exist yet), and until it is, the whole Reports area fails — the publication page, the draft page, and the Reports index for any published Report Definition (#647).
